Paracetamol (Acetaminophen) is used to treat mild to moderate pain and fever. The standard adult dose is 500mg to 1000mg every 4-6 hours, not exceeding 4000mg per day. Overdose can cause serious liver damage.

Ibuprofen is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) used for pain, fever, and inflammation. Standard adult dose is 200-400mg every 4-6 hours with food. Should be avoided by patients with stomach ulcers or kidney problems.

Amoxicillin is a common antibiotic used to treat bacterial infections including chest infections, urinary tract infections, and dental infections. It should be taken for the full prescribed course even if symptoms improve.

Omeprazole is used to treat acid reflux, stomach ulcers, and heartburn. It works by reducing stomach acid production. Common side effects include headache, diarrhea, and nausea.

Cetirizine is an antihistamine used to treat allergies, hay fever, and hives. It causes less drowsiness than older antihistamines. Standard dose is 10mg once daily.
