High blood pressure (hypertension) is a condition where the force of blood against artery walls is consistently too high. Normal blood pressure is below 120/80 mmHg.

Hypertension is often called the silent killer because it has no symptoms but can lead to serious complications including heart attack, stroke, and kidney disease.

Common medications for hypertension include Lisinopril (ACE inhibitor), Amlodipine (calcium channel blocker), and Losartan (ARB). These help relax blood vessels and reduce blood pressure.

Lifestyle changes that help manage blood pressure include reducing salt intake, regular exercise, maintaining a healthy weight, limiting alcohol, quitting smoking, and managing stress.

Patients on blood pressure medication should monitor their blood pressure regularly and never stop medication without consulting their doctor.
