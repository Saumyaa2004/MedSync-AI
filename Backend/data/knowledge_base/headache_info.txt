Headaches are one of the most common health complaints. They can be classified as tension headaches, migraines, or cluster headaches.

Tension headaches cause mild to moderate pain and feel like a tight band around the head. Common triggers include stress, poor posture, and dehydration. Treatment includes over-the-counter pain relievers like paracetamol or ibuprofen, rest, and hydration.

Migraines are severe headaches often accompanied by nausea, vomiting, and sensitivity to light and sound. Common migraine triggers include hormonal changes, certain foods, stress, and sleep disruption. Medications used for migraines include sumatriptan, ibuprofen, and preventive medications like topiramate.

Patients experiencing sudden severe headaches, headaches with fever and stiff neck, or headaches after head injury should seek immediate medical attention as these may indicate serious conditions.

For mild headaches, patients are advised to rest in a quiet dark room, stay hydrated, apply a cold or warm compress, and avoid screen time.
